Apple Pay automation setup

PRO and introductory access can automatically record Apple Pay purchases in your budget. In FREE, events appear in the log but do not become expenses. Manual entry is available without PRO.

  1. Open Shortcuts → Automation → +. Find Wallet and select your cards.
  2. Select the categories you need, choose Run Immediately, and tap Next.
  3. Under Get Started, select the first tile to create a new blank shortcut. Do not choose the ready-made Spend or Grow tile before entering the editor.
  4. Inside the editor, search actions for Spend or Grow → Add Expense.
  5. For the first field, select Shortcut Input, tap the variable, and select its Amount property. Repeat for the second field with the Merchant property. Do not type these words manually.
  6. Confirm the action and save the automation. Keep only one Spend or Grow automation enabled for the same cards.
  7. After a purchase, check expense history and the automation log. Detailed screenshots are in the app menu → Automation setup (Apple Pay).

Backups and changing phones

Open backups from the app menu, export a file, and choose Save to Files → iCloud Drive. On another iPhone with access to the file, choose Restore from Backup and import the JSON file. Check the copy before removing the old app. Automatic cloud backup is not available yet.

Purchases and restoring PRO

Menu → Access and PRO provides plans, purchase restoration, and active subscription management. Use the corresponding Apple Account. Restoring a purchase restores PRO access, not the financial database; that requires a backup.

Core calculations and tracking work on your device offline. Purchases, update checks, and files in iCloud Drive require the relevant services to be available.
