3. Nature of calculations

Results are estimates based on your inputs and the adopted calculation assumptions, not individual financial, investment, legal, or tax advice. The app does not promise returns or guarantee reaching a goal.

The nominal rate, contribution frequency, and compounding affect results. A rate labeled nominal must not be interpreted as a bank’s effective annual rate. Historical return examples do not guarantee future results or eliminate the risk of loss. Currency and currency risk also matter.

Averaging recurring weekly spending or contributions uses 4.33 weeks per month; the result does not equal the number of calendar days in a particular month. Actual bank schedules, fees, taxes, and changes in rates and inflation may differ from the model. Check actual terms before making a financial decision.

For goals, the actual amount for a month means the total accumulated balance, not an individual contribution. You enter it yourself; it is not bank-verified.
