Privacy Policy

Revision and effective date: 24 October 2026.

1. Developer and contact

Spend or Grow is developed by Assylan Aliakparov, an individual, Kazakhstan. For data and privacy enquiries: spendorgrow@icloud.com.

This policy describes the current iPhone app. The app does not require a separate Spend or Grow account and does not connect directly to your bank accounts.

2. Data on your device

The app stores data you enter or import locally:

  • expenses, income, amounts, dates, categories, notes, and merchant names;
  • budgets, periods, planned payments, and their statuses;
  • goals, starting amounts, calculation parameters, and actual savings balances;
  • saved purchase comparisons and calculation parameters;
  • language, currency, appearance, reminder, and category-rule settings;
  • records of active months used for introductory access;
  • local backups and the automation execution log;
  • local counters for manual entries and Apple Pay setup prompts, the last prompt date, your decision to dismiss prompts, and a flag indicating receipt of an Apple Pay transaction.

This processing is necessary for tracking, calculations, displaying results, reminders, recovery, and actions you select. The developer has no remote access to this database. The app has no developer-operated server for uploading your finances and no built-in iCloud synchronization of financial records across devices.

This does not exclude system iPhone backups: their creation and storage depend on device settings and the Apple services you choose.

3. Apple Pay automation and Shortcuts

If you configure an automation yourself, the app receives the amount and merchant name passed by it. Currency comes from your budget settings; the entry’s date and time are the time the action executes. Categories are determined locally.

The app does not receive your bank card number, security code, bank password, or complete Wallet transaction history. The automation is not a bank statement: its completeness and accuracy depend on configuration and iOS operation.

For diagnostics, the device retains a log of the latest 50 executions: received values, receipt time, and processing result. It is not sent to the developer automatically. You can clear the log in the app and disable the automation in Shortcuts.

Automatic entry into the budget is available with PRO and during introductory access. In FREE, received information may still be stored in the log, but does not become budget expenses. Activating PRO does not automatically add previously uncounted entries to the budget. Clearing the log does not remove expenses already saved in the budget.

To avoid repeatedly suggesting setup, the app locally remembers that it has received an Apple Pay transaction. This flag contains no card credentials and survives clearing the log. Setup prompts appear no more than three times and no more than once per day; you can opt out immediately. Counters and your opt-out are used only on the device and are not sent to the developer.

4. Checking for updates

The app requests a version configuration hosted on GitHub Pages. The request uses the current time to refresh the cache; amounts, categories, goals, and notes are not included in that request.

The hosting provider receives technical request information, including the IP address. GitHub states that it logs Pages visitors’ IP addresses for security. Its processing is described in the GitHub privacy statement. Local financial storage does not mean there are no network requests or technical logs held by external providers.

Core tracking and calculation functions can work offline. Update checks and purchases require access to the relevant external services.

5. Purchases and PRO access

Apple processes payments through the App Store. The app uses StoreKit to retrieve product information and verify purchases and access status, including product type and subscription expiry date where applicable. The app does not receive your payment card credentials or Apple Account password.

Data Apple processes through its services is governed by Apple’s privacy policy.

6. Support enquiries

An email is sent only at your initiative through a mail app. The form asks you to describe the issue and includes the Spend or Grow version and build, device model, iOS version, app language, and system locale. You can edit the message and remove diagnostic information before sending it.

If you send an email, the developer receives the sender’s address, message content, and attachments you include. Do not send passwords, card credentials, documents, or your entire financial database unless necessary for the enquiry. Examples with personal information replaced are preferable.

The support address uses iCloud Mail. Sender and recipient mail services handle delivery and storage; processing may occur outside your country. Correspondence is used to respond to, diagnose, and resolve the specific enquiry, not for advertising.

Correspondence is retained for no more than 12 months after the enquiry is closed, unless longer retention is required by law or to resolve a specific ongoing dispute.

7. Export and backups

At your request, the app creates a report or backup and lets you choose its save location or recipient using iOS system tools. The file may contain personal financial information. Cloud drives, mail services, and other apps you choose process the files under their own rules.

You can manually save a backup file to iCloud Drive through Files if iCloud Drive is configured and available on the device. On another iPhone with access to that file, you can select it for recovery through Spend or Grow’s backup import. Apple provides iCloud Drive storage according to your Apple Account settings and the service’s rules. The developer does not automatically receive a copy of the file.

This is manual file export and import, not automatic Spend or Grow cloud backup or cross-device synchronization. The current app does not independently upload backups to iCloud or restore them without an action you select.

JSON backups are not protected by a separate Spend or Grow password. The app’s PIN lock does not protect a file already exported. Keep copies in a trusted location and do not share them with unauthorized people.

The app also creates local automatic backups when data changes, retaining up to five such copies. A local safety copy of previous data may be saved before a restore. Deleting an individual expense does not change backups or reports already created or sent.

8. PIN, biometrics, and notifications

When protection is enabled, the app stores PIN verification data in the system Keychain. The PIN itself is not stored in plain text. iOS performs biometric verification; the app receives the result, not a face image or fingerprint template. These mechanisms do not promise separate encryption of every app file and do not replace protection of the device itself.

If you allow reminders, the app schedules local payment notifications. They may contain payment names and amounts visible on the lock screen depending on iOS settings. You can disable reminders and their content display in app and device settings.

9. Tracking and data sharing

The app offers optional Google Analytics for Firebase. It is off by default. Firebase is not initialized until you explicitly choose “Help improve the app” on the welcome screen or in About. Refusing does not restrict features; consent can be withdrawn in About. Events before consent are not queued for later transmission.

After enabling, Google receives events for opening sections, performing a calculation (at most once per calculator per launch), opening the Apple Pay guide or PRO offer, starting and completing a PRO purchase, and successfully recording an automated expense. Our event parameters are limited to the section name; amounts, merchants, financial entries, comments and goal names are not sent. The SDK may automatically record purchases of PRO itself, including product identifier, price and currency; this is not the content of your budget.

Firebase also processes an app installation identifier, sessions, device and app version information, event times, and approximate location derived from the network connection. This is not fully anonymous data. IDFA, IDFV, ad personalization and automatic screen reporting are disabled. Google may process data outside your country under its privacy policy. Event retention depends on the Google Analytics property settings. Turning analytics off stops further collection and resets local Analytics data, but does not promise immediate deletion of all previously sent data from Google’s servers. Contact the developer with deletion questions. The developer does not sell entered financial data to advertising networks.

This does not exclude the processing by Apple, GitHub, and mail providers described above, or exports you choose yourself. During TestFlight testing, Apple may process crash reports and feedback under TestFlight’s separate rules.

10. Retention, deletion, and your requests

Financial records remain on your device until deleted or changed. To remove related information, check not only records but also local backups, the automation log, exported files, and copies in other services. Removing the app does not delete support emails, files outside the app, App Store data, or existing system backups. Keychain data may persist according to iOS behavior.

The developer cannot remotely delete your local database or restore it without a copy you provide. For questions about access, correction, or deletion of information you sent to support, email spendorgrow@icloud.com. Requests are considered under applicable law and mandatory retention grounds. You do not need to send identity documents with your initial request.

The app does not create a Spend or Grow account, so there is no separate account deletion operation. Removing the app does not cancel an Apple subscription: manage it separately in Apple Account settings.

11. Minors and requests from parents

Spend or Grow is intended for a general audience and is not positioned as an app specifically intended for children. The app does not ask for a date of birth during ordinary use. This does not mean children’s data protection requirements do not apply.

If you are a parent or legal representative and believe a child has provided personal data to the developer, for example in a support email, contact spendorgrow@icloud.com. Identify the message or information concerned; do not include the child’s documents or additional financial information in the initial request.

The developer will review the request and, if necessary, ask for minimal information to verify your authority and locate the enquiry. Further processing, obtaining necessary consent, and deletion are governed by applicable law. Data no longer needed and not subject to mandatory retention must be deleted. The developer cannot remotely view or delete local financial records on the child’s device.

12. Changes to this policy

The policy must be updated before changes to data flows are introduced. The revision date is stated in the document. If new processing requires a separate notice or consent, changing this policy alone does not replace it.
